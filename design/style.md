# Style Guide

## Brand Feel

Rokk Lab should feel modern, precise, editorial, and confident. The tone is polished but approachable: salon expertise with a clean, fashion-forward edge.

## Palette

Use black, white, and neutral grays as the main palette. Use orange accents sparingly for section labels, dividers, active states, icons, and calls to action. Avoid introducing unrelated accent colors unless the brand direction changes.

## Typography

Headlines are uppercase, heavy-weight, and tightly composed. Use bold uppercase eyebrow labels with generous tracking for section context. Body copy should stay compact, clear, and readable, usually in neutral gray on light backgrounds or neutral gray on dark backgrounds.

## Layout

Favor full-width sections, strong alignment, and generous whitespace. Keep page content constrained with existing `max-w-*` patterns. Use responsive grids for services, contact panels, and multi-column editorial sections. Avoid decorative card-heavy layouts unless the content is a repeated item, such as service entries.

## Imagery

Images should show the salon, hair, or service atmosphere directly. Existing image treatments include grayscale editorial imagery, masked hero images, and soft gradient blends into neutral backgrounds. Do not use generic stock-like imagery when a salon-specific asset is available.

## Components

Buttons should feel sharp, direct, and high-contrast, using borders, uppercase labels, and the established arrow motif where appropriate. Service icons should remain consistent in size, spacing, and treatment. Repeated service groups should preserve clear category hierarchy.

## Motion

Motion should be subtle: page fade, section rise, image soft-in, and small hover lifts. Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Avoid large animated effects that distract from booking and service discovery.

## Responsive Rules

Design mobile first, then expand to desktop grids. Keep text from overflowing buttons, tabs, and cards. Preserve scannability on small screens by stacking panels, reducing horizontal decoration, and keeping touch targets comfortable.
